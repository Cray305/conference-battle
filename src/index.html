How we count

Membership

Each game counts for the conferences both teams belonged to that season. Texas's 2023 games count for the Big 12, and its 2024 games count for the SEC. Switch to current membership to regroup every game by where teams play today.

Postseason

Bowl and College Football Playoff games count and can be filtered on their own. Conference title games are played between members of the same conference, so they never appear.

FCS

Every FCS program is grouped into one opponent. Hide the FCS chip to leave those games out of every total. Games against Division II and III teams aren't counted.

Independents

Notre Dame, UConn, and other independents share one row. Games between two independents aren't counted.

Results

Games count as played on the field. Wins the NCAA later vacated still count, and home, away, and neutral-site games are weighted the same.

Small samples

Matchups with fewer than four games are hatched in the matrix. Treat those records as anecdotes, not trends.
